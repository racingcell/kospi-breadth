Looking ahead, if government initiatives to boost market conditions and favorable economic growth materialize effectively, market breadth could improve as investor confidence broadens beyond the largest tech and auto names. Conversely, continued concentration of gains in a few sectors could maintain the divergence between headline indices and overall breadth indicators. Monitoring shifts in the A-D line and moving average participation will be important for assessing market internals in the coming weeks.
